Autonomous Personal Load Carrier (APLC)

An independent-study research concept for a mobile robot that carries loads between a vehicle and a user's home, including ramps, stairs, thresholds and indoor spaces. It is in the brainstorming phase.

Project specConcept
Context
Independent study at Johns Hopkins University under Prof. Anton Dahbura
Role
Working on the mechanical and electrical systems, with a focus on developing the robot's chassis, cargo-handling and vehicle-docking mechanisms, actuation, sensing interfaces, and safety considerations.
Team
Collaborating with subteams such as security, perception, electrical and mechanical

Overview

The concept

01

The APLC is a research concept for a mobile robot designed to transport loads between a vehicle and a user's home, including navigating ramps, stairs, thresholds, and indoor spaces.

The project explores the mechanical, electrical, sensing, and autonomous systems needed to safely handle varying environments and loads.

Done so far

Current stage

02

Concept and brainstorming phase. No hardware has been built yet; the current work focuses on system architecture, mechanism concepts, sensing requirements, and feasibility.
