PROJECT 01 JHU · EN.530.691 · 2025

Grasping Force Feedback for da Vinci Surgical Robots

A 3D-printed, 1:1.2 scale replica of the da Vinci leader-hand gripper, driven by a capstan mechanism and a Hapkit 3.0 board, renders grasping forces that let users tell free space, soft tissue and hard tissue apart. Measured forces: free space 0-0.05 N, soft tissue 1-3 N, hard tissue 2-4 N.

Role · team of 4Project lead: gripper CAD design, 3D printing, prototyping; co-developed the board code for the tissue states.

  • Haptics
  • Capstan drive
  • Hapkit 3.0
  • C++
  • CAD
  • 3D printing

PROJECT 02 JHU · EN.530.646 · 2025

UR5e Autonomous Push-and-Place Manipulation

A UR5e arm, taught a start pose in freedrive mode, autonomously pushes a foam cube about 16 cm along a straight line and back. Inverse-kinematics and resolved-rate Cartesian control were compared on the real robot through ROS 2 and MATLAB RTDE; IK position error at the waypoints was 0.001485–0.00712 mm, against 5.259–10.000 mm for RR.

Role · 2-person core teamRobotics Control Engineer: controls, ROS 2 and MATLAB RTDE implementation, hardware execution; designed the IK solution filter.

  • ROS 2
  • MATLAB RTDE
  • Inverse kinematics
  • Resolved-rate control
  • RViz

PROJECT 03 JHU · EN.520.427 · 2025

Clarifeye: Affordable AI-Based Fundus Imaging

A fully mechanical, 3D-printed handheld attachment designed to align any smartphone camera with a 20D condenser lens for retinal imaging, using X–Y slider alignment and threaded axial focus. It is the hardware half of a course startup concept for eye screening in low- and middle-income countries.

Role · team of 4Hardware Design Lead: all CAD, 3D printing, prototyping and assembly; patent search. No part in the software.

  • SolidWorks

PROJECT 04 UIC Senior Design · 2023–24

Adaptable Pedal Box for FSAE Combustion and Electric Cars

A pedal box on a single mountable baseplate, so the same unit fits both UIC's combustion and electric Formula SAE cars. It was simulated at the 2 kN FSAE load case, iterated to reduce brake pedal mass from 304.54 g to 191.25 g, and won Best in Show (Mechanical & Industrial) at the UIC Engineering Expo.

Role · team of 4Mechanical Design & Prototyping Engineer: majority of the CAD; all prototyping and assembly.
